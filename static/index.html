    Keys
    • Space plays and pauses
    • , and . step one frame back and forward. Left and right do the same, and Shift steps one second
    • Shift+, and Shift+. jump to the previous and next keyframe
    • Alt and the arrows nudge the box, Shift nudges by 10 pixels
    • [ and ] set the start and end
    • K sets a keyframe, Delete removes it
    • Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Y redoes
    • − and + magnify the picture. The wheel zooms toward the cursor. Middle-drag, right-drag, or Alt-drag pans. 0 resets
    • The wheel over the timeline zooms it, Shift-wheel pans it, Trim shows the start-to-end range, and \ fits the whole clip
